About us

Local folks,
local radio

SoMdMesh is a volunteer and hobbyist-run group in Southern Maryland.

People behind the radios

Built on interest.
Run by volunteers.

We’re brought together by an interest in Meshtastic, radio technology, and off-grid communication. SoMdMesh is a place for learning, experimenting, and connecting with fellow hobbyists.

Our group runs on the time and enthusiasm of volunteers. Whether you’re discovering mesh networking or already enjoy working with radios, that shared curiosity is what brings us together.

Get In Contact

If you have a cool project you'd like to feature on the site, please get in touch at somdmesh@gmail.com.

One thing to keep in mind is that the mesh networks are decentralized and are not controlled by us, we can only help to share information and connect folks together. It's like the early internet ;)
