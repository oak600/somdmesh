What is Meshtastic?

Meshtastic is an open-source project that uses small, low-power LoRa radios to send messages without relying on cellular service or an internet connection.

Compatible devices form a mesh network, allowing messages to travel between radios and be relayed by other nodes. It’s a hands-on way to explore radio technology and community-built communication.
